\begin{abstract}
SharpeArena is a point-in-time reinforcement-learning environment for trading agents whose interface excludes future-bar access, whose Rust core is pinned byte-for-byte by golden fingerprints its native, WebAssembly and Python surfaces each assert, and whose trajectories are recomputed from recorded decisions. Agents use a governed JSON contract; procedural scenarios occupy disjoint train, gap and held-out seed bands; ranking is delegated to SharpeBench. The experiments calibrate these instruments rather than make market claims. The canonical generator fails its own finite-panel-calibrated realism diagnostic on 23 of 24 seeded panels; an opt-in volatility-clustering driver improves that clustering, but no Calm setting survives a predeclared diagnostic, confirmation and volatility-constrained calibration rule, while the conditional-IID-calibrated Fano statistic remains exploratory. Fixed-spread market making exhibits a U-shaped sampled-grid regret curve against the Avellaneda--Stoikov reference; its minimum is not statistically distinguishable from the reference at 16 episodes, which is not evidence of equivalence. A normalized-flow concave-impact ablation makes the manipulation probe falsifiable. An exploratory 135-cell asymmetric search finds a best no-follower cell of $+21.8\times10^{-4}$; its pointwise 95\% interval $[17.4,26.2]\times10^{-4}$ remains positive after a Bonferroni familywise correction, $[10.0,33.6]\times10^{-4}$, and a fixed-cell 32-seed replication gives $+26.3\times10^{-4}$, $[23.7,28.9]\times10^{-4}$. A 60-cell extension, corrected over the global 195-cell family, contains 23 positive, 31 negative and 6 unresolved intervals; all 45 sampled linear-impact cells have negative means, finite-grid results rather than universal claims. In an endogenous adverse-selection arm, informed-flow maker markout at 20 bars falls from $+0.095$ at sampled $\lambda=0.2$ to $-0.196$ at $\lambda=0.4$, while the paired informed--uninformed gap stays positive; the six-value sweep is exploratory and does not estimate a crossover. An honest autoregressive adversary remains near its unconditional baseline, while a public $2^{16}$ seed band is recovered 16/16 times; an opt-in sealed derivation defeats that specific scanner 0/16 when its salt is withheld, and the companion forward protocol can commit the salt hash before entry. An out-of-band oracle witness, using common random numbers and monotonicity checks, shows that eligibility is attainable on every tier and that the every-seed probabilistic-Sharpe-ratio (PSR) gate is the last to open at every attained crossing of all ten eligible cells, replicated over five independent noise paths each. Finally, an eight-seed ecology replication finds shock-driven winner replacement in 1 of 8 seeds, so a single-trajectory reading of the same probe does not survive replication.
\end{abstract}

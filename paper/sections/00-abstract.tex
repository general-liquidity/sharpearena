\begin{abstract}
SharpeArena is a point-in-time reinforcement-learning environment for trading agents whose interface excludes future-bar access, whose Rust core is pinned byte-for-byte across native, WebAssembly and Python bindings, and whose trajectories are recomputed from recorded decisions. Agents use a governed JSON contract; procedural scenarios occupy disjoint train, gap and held-out seed bands; ranking is delegated to SharpeBench. The experiments calibrate these instruments rather than make market claims. The canonical generator fails its own realism gate on 22 of 24 seeded panels; an opt-in volatility-persistence driver improves clustering, while the finite-panel Fano statistic is now reported only as exploratory because its null is not calibrated. Fixed-spread market making exhibits a U-shaped sampled-grid regret curve against the Avellaneda--Stoikov reference; its minimum is not statistically distinguishable from the reference at 16 episodes, which is not evidence of equivalence. A normalized-flow concave-impact ablation makes the manipulation probe falsifiable. An exploratory 135-cell asymmetric search finds a best no-follower cell of $+21.8\times10^{-4}$; its pointwise 95\% interval $[17.4,26.2]\times10^{-4}$ remains positive after a Bonferroni familywise correction, $[10.0,33.6]\times10^{-4}$. All 45 sampled linear-impact cells have negative means, a finite-grid result rather than a universal claim. An honest autoregressive adversary remains near its unconditional baseline, while a public $2^{16}$ seed band is recovered 16/16 times; an opt-in sealed derivation defeats that specific scanner 0/16 when its salt is withheld, and the companion forward protocol can commit the salt hash before entry. An out-of-band oracle witness, using common random numbers and monotonicity checks, shows that eligibility is attainable on every tier and that the every-seed PSR conjunction is last to open at all ten observed thresholds. Finally, an eight-seed ecology replication overturns its original single-seed headline: shock-driven winner replacement occurs in one seed, not seven.
\end{abstract}

\section{Limitations}\label{sec:limitations}

\paragraph{The evidence run has not executed.} Every number in \cref{sec:experiments} is pending: the experiments are defined, seeded and committed as scripts, but the scoring kernel is being upgraded and the run waits for the integrated version. The design claims of \cref{sec:leakfree,sec:determinism,sec:replay,sec:contract} are properties of committed code and tests and stand on their own; the empirical claims do not exist yet, and the paper says so rather than projecting them.

\paragraph{All markets are synthetic.} The generator is calibrated by construction, not by data: no real price series enters the environment. The realism gate certifies directional stylized facts, which is a necessary condition for believable tape and far from a sufficient one, and two of its six facts, skew and Zumbach asymmetry, are informational rather than gated because their sign is asset-dependent. An agent that excels here has demonstrated skill against this generator's structure, not against a market.

\paragraph{The impact models are stylized.} The endogenous market composes linear Kyle and Almgren-Chriss terms; the adverse-selection module deliberately does not separate the meta-order's information from its impact; the manipulation probe's follower population is a one-parameter caricature of momentum chasing. F5 and F6 are designed to measure where these specifications break, and a boundary they fail to find is reported as a defect of the environment, but a stylized model can also fail realistically for the wrong reason.

\paragraph{The guard is a deny-list.} The structural guarantee covers the environment's own surface. \texttt{LookaheadGuard} extends it to harness tools by pattern, and a deny-list is exactly as good as its patterns; a novel leak path through a side channel the environment does not own, wall-clock time, filesystem state, a network call, is out of scope for both layers. The replay check verifies decisions against frozen data and therefore catches falsified results, not information an agent obtained elsewhere.

\paragraph{Determinism is pinned where it is claimed.} The byte-identical guarantee covers the Rust core and its WebAssembly and Python bindings, where golden hashes and parity tests enforce it. The Python probe layer, ecology, adverse selection, manipulation, realism, is deterministic in its seeds through NumPy's seeded generators but is off the golden-hash path, and float reproducibility there is the platform's, not the paper's.

\paragraph{No hosted arena.} The contract, the conformance kit and the replay verifier exist; a hosted intake, a live leaderboard and multi-tenant isolation of untrusted agent processes do not. The governance document is a written promise backed by tests, and it has not yet been tested by a hostile or careless third-party implementer.

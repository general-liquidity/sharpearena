\subsection{Extended sweeps through the positive-control winner}\label{sec:f5-extended-sweeps}

The positive control leaves push weight, $\lambda$, total leg length, hold length,
short-side trips and follower gain outside its initial grid. We sweep each axis one at a
time through its no-follower, zero-temporary-impact, uniform $9{:}1$ winner at
$\beta\in\{1,0.5\}$, five values per axis and eight common seeds: 60 cells. Because the
anchor was selected in the earlier 135-cell grid on these same seeds, the familywise
intervals are two-sided Bonferroni 95\% $t$ intervals over the global 195-cell family
(the antecedent grid plus this extension), not over 60 cells alone. Importantly,
unequal ``uniform'' legs use separate $1/n_{\rm up}$ and $1/n_{\rm down}$ increments, and tests verify that each
leg executes exactly one signed peak notional. Thus changing duration no longer silently
changes the amount liquidated.

The corrected results are still sharply asymmetric by exponent, but remain a finite grid.
Across the 60 extension cells, 23 global-familywise intervals are positive, 31 are
negative and 6 cross zero. The longest tested $45{:}5$ theory-arm trip is the largest
observed cell, $+135.0\times10^{-4}$ with global-familywise interval
$[122.1,148.0]\times10^{-4}$; this is an observed finite-grid maximum, not an optimum.
The result expands the diagnostic's search coverage, not its economic scope: interactions,
other exponents, other flow scales, transient impact and overshooting round trips remain
untested (\cref{fig:f5-extended-sweeps}).

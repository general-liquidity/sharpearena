\subsection{Exploratory asymmetric-schedule positive control}\label{sec:f5-positive-control}

Concavity makes slicing matter. For positive dimensionless flow $x=Q/V$, the sum of the $n$ per-slice impact increments before the engine's multiplicative price compounding is
\[
n I_\kappa(Q/n;V)=n^{1-\kappa} I_\kappa(Q;V),
\]
so a slowly accumulated leg and a block liquidation need not cancel when $\kappa<1$. The identity is about the impact function, not the compounded terminal price. It motivates a positive control; it does not guarantee that one of our finite schedules must profit after compounding, temporary costs and follower flow.

The search crosses five up/down duration ratios, three block fractions, three exponents and three market arms: 135 cells, each on eight seeds. All displayed cell intervals are pointwise. Because the reported winner is selected after seeing the grid, its headline inference uses a two-sided Bonferroni familywise 95\% interval over all 135 cells (Student $t$, 7 degrees of freedom); the evidence stores the family size, critical value and adjusted interval.

The best observed cell is the no-follower, zero-temporary-impact arm at $\kappa=0.5$: nine bars of uniform accumulation followed by one-bar liquidation yield $+21.8\times10^{-4}$. Its pointwise 95\% interval is $[17.4,26.2]\times10^{-4}$ and its familywise interval remains positive at $[10.0,33.6]\times10^{-4}$. The same sampled schedule with temporary impact yields $+18.4\times10^{-4}$, with familywise interval $[6.4,30.4]\times10^{-4}$. The canonical follower arm has no pointwise-positive cell. At $\kappa=0.7$ no sampled mean is positive. Among the 45 sampled linear cells, every mean is negative; the $1{:}9$ and $9{:}1$ theory-arm values are numerically near-symmetric, not bit-identical. These statements concern the sampled grid only.

The positive control shows that this instrument can detect a profitable sampled round trip under normalized concave impact. It does not establish a universal region, an optimum, or a theorem-level correspondence: the search fixes push size, $\lambda$, $V$, total leg length and most follower gains, and omits short-side, overshooting and longer-hold trips. Conversely, the absence of a positive among 45 sampled linear cells is consistent with the cited linear-impact model under its assumptions, not empirical proof about every configuration of the product.

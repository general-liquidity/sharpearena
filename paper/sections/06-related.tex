\section{Related work}\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Environment interfaces.} Gym \citep{brockman2016gym} demonstrated that a small, stable environment interface is worth more than any single environment, and Gymnasium \citep{towers2024gymnasium} carried the interface forward with versioned environment IDs and a conformance checker; SharpeArena's Python surface is a first-class Gymnasium citizen with versioned, namespaced IDs and adopts its \texttt{check\_env}. PettingZoo \citep{terry2021pettingzoo} standardized the multi-agent case, which SharpeArena's shared-book and batched-competition environments implement. Minari \citep{minari} standardizes offline-RL datasets; SharpeArena exports leak-safe rollouts to it. The wire contract of \cref{sec:contract} differs from all of these in scope: it is a language-agnostic JSON boundary for external agent processes, governed like a protocol rather than a library API.

\paragraph{Evaluation rigor in RL.} The environment's evaluation stance imports three lessons from the RL evaluation literature. From the ALE revisitation \citep{machado2018revisiting}: determinism in the environment invites trajectory memorization, so evaluation must be designed against it; SharpeArena keeps determinism for verifiability and defeats memorization with procedural scenario distributions and held-out seed bands instead of injected stochasticity. From Procgen \citep{cobbe2020procgen}: procedural generation over integer seed intervals makes generalization measurable, which \cref{sec:scenarios} ports to markets. Prioritized Level Replay \citep{jiang2021prioritized} shows seed-level curricula shape what agents learn, which motivates keeping the train band operator-owned and the held-out band frozen. $\tau$-bench \citep{yao2024taubench} argues that agent reliability must be measured across repeated runs rather than on average; that requirement lives in the SharpeBench kernel this environment feeds \citep{toca2026sharpebench}.

\paragraph{Market simulation.} ABIDES \citep{byrd2020abides} is the closest simulator in spirit: a high-fidelity multi-agent market with a matching engine and background agent populations. SharpeArena trades some of that fidelity for properties ABIDES does not target: cross-runtime byte-determinism with committed golden hashes, a structurally leak-free observation boundary, replay-based tamper evidence, and a governed external-agent wire contract. The microstructure models are the classical ones: Kyle's permanent impact \citep{kyle1985continuous}, Almgren-Chriss temporary impact and execution cost \citep{almgren2001optimal}, and the Avellaneda-Stoikov quoting model with its closed-form optimum \citep{avellaneda2008high}. The realism gate certifies the generator against Cont's stylized facts \citep{cont2001empirical}.

\paragraph{Scoring.} The environment deliberately contains no scorer. The deflated Sharpe \citep{bailey2014deflated} and the reliability and process gates that consume this environment's trajectories are specified in the SharpeBench paper \citep{toca2026sharpebench}; this paper is the producer half of that funnel.
